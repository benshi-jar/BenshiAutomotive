Known weak points

Timing chain and oil pump chain guides

Plastic guides break up, the chain skips, and the engine can be destroyed with little warning. Often starts as a whine from the front of the engine. Class action covered 2012-2015 model years. BMW extended chain coverage to 7 yr / 70k, with partial help to 8 yr / 100k. That coverage has expired on age for these cars, so ask for proof the chain was replaced. Cost range is the settlement's caps: $3,000 for the chain job, $7,500 for a failed engine.

$3,000 to $7,500

Owners also report: valve cover and gasket leaks, oil filter housing gasket leaks, electric water pump.

Prices are what a repair shop typically charges, as quoted by the source below. "Get a quote" means the source gave no figure.

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the timing chain and oil pump chain guides?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?
