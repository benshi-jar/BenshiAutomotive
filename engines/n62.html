Engine guides / N62

BMW N62 problems: what breaks and what it costs

Buyer risk: high4.4L / 4.8L V8, no turboBuilt 2001-2010

Cheap to buy, expensive to keep. Three known leaks that each cost four figures at a shop.

Which cars have the N62

E60 545i, 550iE63/E64 645Ci, 650iE65/E66 745i, 750iE53 X5 4.4i, 4.8isE70 X5 4.8i

Known weak points

Valve stem seals

Blue-white smoke on cold start or after idling, heavy oil use. Independent shop with the special tool. Used to be $5,000 to $10,000.

$1,800 to $3,000
Coolant transfer pipe

Coolant leaks from a pipe buried in the engine valley. $4,000+ if done the factory way.

$1,000 to $2,500
Alternator bracket gasket

Oil leak at the front of the engine.

$1,000 to $1,500
Valve cover gaskets

Oil leak and burning oil smell.

$700 to $1,000

Prices are what a repair shop typically charges, as quoted by the source below. "Get a quote" means the source gave no figure.

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the valve stem seals?
  • Do you have a receipt for the coolant transfer pipe?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?

Source: bmwtuning.co. Compiled 2026-11-16.
