BMW S55 problems: what breaks and what it costs

Buyer risk: medium3.0L inline-six, twin turbo (M2 Competition, M3, M4)Built 2014-2020

Solid for an M engine. The famous crank hub issue is rare on stock cars.

Which cars have the S55

F80 M3 (2015-2018)F82/F83 M4 (2015-2020)F87 M2 Competition (2019-2021)

Known weak points

Crank hub slip

Sudden limp mode and rough running when the timing slips. Source puts reports under 1% of cars. More of a concern on tuned cars. Ask if the car has been tuned.

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the crank hub slip?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?
