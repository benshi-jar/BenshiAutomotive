Engine guides / N54

BMW N54 problems: what breaks and what it costs

Buyer risk: high3.0L inline-six, twin turboBuilt 2006-2016 (most US cars 2007-2010)

Fast and loved by tuners, but the most repair-prone six here. Buy only with records.

Which cars have the N54

E90/E92/E93 335i (2007-2010)E60 535i (2008-2010)E82/E88 135i (2008-2010)E89 Z4 35i, 35isE92 335isE82 1M

Known weak points

Turbo wastegate rattle and turbo failure

Rattle, low boost codes, power loss, smoke. $3,500 independent, $6,000 dealer per source. Extended turbo warranty (10 yr / 82k) has long expired.

$3,500 to $6,000
High pressure fuel pump

Long crank, limp mode. Fails at random. Part about $450 plus install. Extended warranty (10 yr / 120k) has expired on age.

Get a quote
Fuel injectors

Hard cold starts, misfires, rough idle. About $1,400 in parts for a set plus coding and labor.

$1,500 and up
Electric water pump

Overheating, rapid coolant loss.

Usually 70,000 to 80,000 miles
$700 to $900
Valve cover and gasket

Oil on plugs and coils, burning oil smell. Dealer labor alone about $1,000 per source.

$340 to $1,300
Carbon build-up on intake valves

Sluggish, misfires, hard starts. Needs walnut blasting.

Usually every 30,000 to 45,000 miles
Get a quote
Plastic charge pipe

Cracks under boost, mostly on tuned cars. Upgraded pipe about $250 in parts.

$250 and up

Prices are what a repair shop typically charges, as quoted by the source below. "Get a quote" means the source gave no figure.

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the turbo wastegate rattle and turbo failure?
  • Do you have a receipt for the high pressure fuel pump?
  • Do you have a receipt for the fuel injectors?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?

Source: bmwtuning.co. Compiled 2026-11-16.
