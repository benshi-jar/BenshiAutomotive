Engine guides / N55

BMW N55 problems: what breaks and what it costs

Buyer risk: medium3.0L inline-six, single turboBuilt 2009-2019 (US from 2010-2011)

Fairly reliable, with a predictable list of gasket and cooling repairs around 80,000 to 100,000 miles.

Known weak points

Valve cover and gasket

Oil on plugs, burning oil smell. The PCV valve is built into the cover and fails with it. $1,000 independent, $1,300 dealer per source.

Usually 80,000 to 100,000 miles
$1,000 to $1,300
Electric water pump and thermostat

Sudden overheating with little warning.

Usually about 100,000 on average, some as early as 30,000 to 40,000 miles
$890 to $950
Oil filter housing gasket

Oil drips onto the belt tensioner and drive belt. Replace the whole housing on cars past 100,000 miles.

$410 to $420
VANOS solenoids

Power loss, hesitation, rough idle. Two solenoids at about $165 each plus about $200 labor.

$530 and up
High pressure fuel pump

Mostly 2010 and early to mid 2011 cars. No extended warranty on this engine.

Get a quote

Owners also report: plastic charge pipe cracking under boost, oil pan gasket leak.

Prices are what a repair shop typically charges, as quoted by the source below. "Get a quote" means the source gave no figure.

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the electric water pump and thermostat?
  • Do you have a receipt for the oil filter housing gasket?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?

Source: bmwtuning.co. Compiled 2026-11-16.
