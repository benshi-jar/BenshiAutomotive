Engine guides / S65

BMW S65 problems: what breaks and what it costs

Buyer risk: high without rod bearing proof4.0L V8, no turbo (M3)Built 2007-2013

A special engine with one big question: have the rod bearings been replaced, and when.

Which cars have the S65

E90 M3 sedan (2008-2011)E92 M3 coupe (2008-2013)E93 M3 convertible (2008-2013)

Known weak points

Rod bearing wear

Bearings wear early. If one fails, the engine is destroyed. Preventive replacement is the norm. Source recommends preventive replacement at 40,000 to 50,000 miles. Cost not in source, needs a shop quote.

Usually 40,000 to 80,000 miles
Get a quote
Throttle actuators

Check engine light, limp mode. Two actuators, labor heavy.

Usually 60,000 to 100,000 miles
Get a quote
VANOS solenoids and seals

Rough idle, lost low-end torque.

Usually 60,000 to 90,000 miles
Get a quote
Oil consumption

Uses oil between changes.

Usually 50,000+ miles

Ask the seller

  • Do you have a receipt for the rod bearing wear?
  • Do you have a receipt for the throttle actuators?
  • How often was the oil changed, and where?
  • Can I take it to an independent BMW shop for an inspection before I buy?
